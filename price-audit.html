Scorecard

What the test shows

Shown prices are mostly current

When a tool showed a price for an item the seller stocks, it matched: Smartprix 8 of 8, Quick Compare 15 of 15. Comparify was right on 12 of 15; its three misses were all Zepto, each ₹1 lower than Zepto's own site.

Missing listings are the real problem

Quick Compare missed Zepto on 6 of the 8 items Zepto sells here. Comparify marks Instamart "Unavailable" at this address and missed all 6 Instamart items that were in stock.

A miss can hide the cheapest option

Zepto had Tata Simply Better groundnut oil 5L at ₹1,549, versus ₹1,789 on Blinkit and Instamart. Smartprix and Quick Compare didn't list Zepto for it, so they would steer you to a price ₹240 higher.

Stock status can be wrong

Smartprix and Quick Compare both showed Amul Gold 500ml as out of stock on Instamart, but Instamart listed it in stock at ₹35.

Smartprix's website caps searches

After about 12 searches it asked me to log in or install the app, so only 4 of the 10 items could be scored there.

No tool splits your list

None of the three takes the whole list and assigns items to stores under each platform's minimum order value. That logic is still yours to build.

How this was tested

  • Each item was pinned to one SKU: Amul Gold pouch 500 ml; Tata Sampann Unpolished Toor Dal 500 g; Dettol Original handwash refill 675 ml; Tata Simply Better cold-pressed groundnut oil 5 L; Amul salted butter 500 g; Amul processed cheese block 200 g; NOICE idli-dosa batter 1 kg; tender coconut (per piece); 24 Mantra Organic idli rice 1 kg; Harpic Original 1 L.
  • "Not listed" by the seller was confirmed by scrolling the full search results, since Zepto and Instamart load results lazily.
  • Blinkit doesn't carry the plain Amul processed cheese block here. Its 200 g block is named "Amul Pizza Processed Cheese Block" at ₹129, and every tool matched that listing, so it is treated as the same SKU.
  • NOICE is Instamart's own label. It was sold out there, and Blinkit and Zepto don't stock it.
  • Two seller answers changed on recheck: Zepto's Harpic 1 L (₹208) and 24 Mantra idli rice (₹142) were listed further down the page than the first screen.
  • Account-specific prices (Zepto Pass, Swiggy One, bank offers) aren't reflected on any side.
  • BigBasket's website wouldn't accept the address in the test browser, so BigBasket figures are shown but not scored. The tools agree with each other there (dal ₹118, oil ₹1,994).
  • BuyHatke wasn't scored: it needs its Chrome extension plus logins to Zepto and Amazon.
